# Request Deletion of Your Data

Last updated: June 2026

Chamate allows users to request deletion of certain personal data without deleting their account.

## How to request data deletion

Send an email to:

[support@neurakind.com](mailto:support@neurakind.com)

Please include:

* Your account email address
* The type of data you would like deleted

## Types of data you may request to delete

Depending on your request, we may delete:

* Conversation history
* Learning progress records
* User-generated content
* Other personal data associated with your account

## Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required for:

* Security
* Fraud prevention
* Abuse prevention
* Legal compliance

Token usage records may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse prevention and service integrity purposes.

## Account remains active

Requesting deletion of specific data does not delete your Chamate account.

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, please visit:

https://neurakind.github.io/Chamate-docs/delete-account
